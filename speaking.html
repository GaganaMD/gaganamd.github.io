01 / SPEAKER PROFILE

Research, learning, and community.

Gagana M D is an AI safety researcher, speaker, workshop facilitator, and community contributor.

Her work connects AI safety research with practical learning: helping people understand evaluation, research pathways, and the technical tools that make a portfolio of work visible. She contributes to Women in Big Data India, serves as a WiDS Ambassador in 2026, and has supported the Women Who Code Data community.

03 / COMMUNITY IMPACT

The Shakti Program.

The Shakti Program is a six-month mentoring and upskilling program. Its reported program-level outcomes include 100% of participants upskilled, an approximately 68% employability or return-to-work outcome, six hours of 1:1 mentoring per participant, and three workshops. These are program outcomes, not individual achievements.
