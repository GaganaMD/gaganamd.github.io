<p>AI control probably won't scale much beyond human-level capability. This initiative asks what has to be in place before that point: how humans and advanced AI systems could cooperate once they are parties we reason and negotiate with rather than tools we direct. It covers trust, verification and credible commitments, and convenes people from AI safety, governance and game theory who mostly haven't met.</p>
<p>I designed and hosted the first roundtable, <a href="speaking.html#roundtable">“What makes cooperation without control work?”</a> (20 October 2026), with about ten participants. It moved from obstacles, to cooperation arrangements, to how each could fail from either side, to two-way verification and testable research claims. Each proposed mechanism was recorded with both sides' incentives, its critical assumption, how it fails and how it could be tested.</p>
