<p>Animals split their choices between options in proportion to how rewarding each one is (Herrnstein's Matching Law). Does a transformer-based RL agent do the same? We trained agents on a two-choice task cued by MNIST digits, varying the reward ratio from 1:1 to 16:1, and compared response ratios with reward ratios on log–log axes. The agents' choice proportions closely tracked the reward ratios, with an approximately linear log–log relationship.</p>
