Research

AI control · Monitoring

Detection Is Not Containment

Measures whether a monitor flags a prohibited action early enough for an intervention to prevent it, then replays the intervention to measure both prevention and the cost of false alarms.

Method
Synthetic authorization environment. Three monitor views (actions; plus reasoning; plus environment telemetry). Local qwen3:14b monitor with a frozen prompt and oracle. Threshold calibrated on benign controls only. Preregistered follow-up phases.
Result
Grounded detection was timely by one step (margin +1). Decisions held in 8 of 12 meaning-preserving identifier renamings. In a closed-loop replay, a false alarm revoked a benign agent's authority and failed its task.
Limits
One environment, single deterministic draws, fixed prompts. Says nothing about chain-of-thought faithfulness or general control performance.
Role
Team of three (with Kaustubh Gupta and Maria Ines). I'm the first-listed author and I maintain the public repository.
Completed · code and data public Apart Research AI Incident Response Sprint, 2026

World models · Evaluation

Trustworthy LLM-generated world models

LLMs are increasingly used to write the environments that agents are trained and tested in. If a generated environment quietly changes the decision structure (which states are reachable, which actions are reversible), any safety conclusion drawn inside it inherits that error.

Method
Evaluation of executable world models across mechanical validity (does it run), structural fidelity (does it encode the intended game), and semantic consistency. Five LLMs, 44 scenarios, 410 generated POSGs.
Why it matters
Downstream safety reasoning about reachability, reversibility and empowerment depends on the environment being right.
Role
Research Fellow, mentored by Jobst Heitzig. Co-author on the write-up.
Fellowship project · write-up not yet public PRISM AI Safety Research Fellowship, May–Oct 2026
Auditing · Hidden objectives

Agent auditing under limited observability

An auditor rarely sees everything an agent did. This line of work asks which conclusions about an agent's objectives survive when traces are incomplete or reconstructed after the fact.

Secret Loyalties
An agent-evaluation setup for hidden loyalties: what an evaluator needs, in affordances and evidence, to recover them.
Agent-Trace
Compares native tool events with reconstructed traces, to measure what is lost before a downstream safety evaluation sees the data.
Status
In progress. No public code or write-up yet, so I'm not reporting results here.

Intervention-aware monitoring

A research direction rather than a result. It asks how authorization boundaries, fail-closed checks, privilege separation and auditable traces could make monitoring of tool-using agents actionable. Detection Is Not Containment tested one narrow version of this idea: revoking a single capability.

Evaluation infrastructure

Synth Benchmark

An offline-first harness for long-horizon agent tasks in finance and due diligence. The engineering choices are the interesting part: there is one canonical task file, task packages are generated rather than hand-edited, and grading is rubric-based with criteria that score precision, recall and F1. Contradictions, unsafe actions and prompt-injection compliance are automatic failures, and a leakage guard refuses to load ground truth if grading assets are visible in the agent's workspace.

Status
Infrastructure and first multi-system tabulations exist. Live side-effect auditing against real systems is not implemented. Mock mode only.

Interpretability

Prompt sensitivity and latent drift in a small causal-reasoning model

Does a model's answer flip when a causal-reasoning prompt is reworded while the causal structure stays the same, and do those flips line up with hidden-state movement? I built synthetic vignettes covering confounding, mediation and selection bias, plus variants that remove a confounder or rename terms. On Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct I ran prompt-stability analysis, layerwise probes and activation contrasts.

Early runs showed high answer flip rates across reframings, and the largest drift with "mechanistic" prompts. The repository is explicit that probe separability and drift are correlational and don't establish mechanism.

Representations

Latent manifold reasoning

A toy study of what happens when a smooth latent trajectory is forced through a narrow, discrete interface. It uses PCA embeddings of the scikit-learn digits data. Early versions had no baselines, and almost any perturbation looked like "collapse". Later versions compare structured routing against isotropic noise and shuffled anchors, with bootstrap intervals and permutation tests.

The result I find most interesting is a mixed one: routing damaged neighbourhood preservation while shortening one path-length measure. One metric isn't enough to judge it. None of this is evidence about real transformer hidden states.

Reinforcement learning

The Matching Law in transformer-based RL agents

A Neuromatch Impact Scholars team project. It asks whether a transformer-based agent trained with Q-learning on a two-choice, MNIST-cued task allocates its choices in proportion to reward rates, as the Matching Law from behavioural psychology predicts.
