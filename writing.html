Tacit Collusion in Pricing Agents: Robustness, Detection, and a Mechanistic Probe of a Pricing LLM

Does a pricing LLM that settles on high prices actually behave like a colluder? One stubbornly competitive rival breaks the habit; inside the model, a steerable trace of the rival's price exists, but it doesn't punish undercutting. High prices alone aren't evidence of collusion.

Research report, with Chewon Cho and Sriram Bharadwaj. I co-led the setup and ran the mechanistic interpretability and steering experiments. Details

Noticing the Move, Missing the Player: LLM Agents Detect Behavioral Shifts but Not Peer Swaps

If an overseer quietly swaps a monitor into a coalition of colluding agents, will the others notice? Across three multi-agent games, agents noticed changes in behaviour but never the replacement itself.

Research report, with Siva Kumar Lakkoju and Kaustubh Gupta. Details
