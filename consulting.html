Consulting

A second pair of eyes on your agent evaluations.

I take on a small amount of technical work next to my research. It's mostly helping teams check whether their evaluation of an AI agent or model supports the conclusions they want to draw from it. The work is scoped and research-shaped, and every area below links to something I've built or studied myself.

Where I can help

Problems worth a look.

Agent evaluation review

Does the evaluation measure what you think it does? I look for grading leaks, missing baselines, thresholds tuned on test data, and claims that go further than the evidence.

Based on: Detection Is Not Containment

Benchmark design and validation

Turning a vague capability question into tasks, rubrics and ground truth that someone else can rerun. That includes deciding which failures should count as automatic fails.

Based on: world-model evaluation and an industry agent benchmark

Monitoring and trace analysis

When does your monitor see the evidence, when does it act, and what does it miss when traces are incomplete or a representation shifts in ways that shouldn't matter?

Based on: agent auditing work and Detection Is Not Containment

Failure analysis

Working through a set of agent or LLM failures to find patterns, separate model failures from harness failures, and write it up so the team can act on it.

Based on: world-model study (134 of 135 generated games ran; most were structurally wrong)

What you'd get

Typical deliverables.

  • A written reviewFindings ranked by how much they affect your conclusions, with concrete fixes.
  • A small reproducible experimentCode and a short report that tests one specific question about your system.
  • A methods briefingA short, sourced summary of how others evaluate a given risk or capability, and where the methods are weak.

Scope

What I don't offer.

I don't provide security certifications, regulatory compliance sign-off, or formal safety guarantees. I'm an early-career researcher, and the most useful thing I can give you is a careful, honest read of your evaluation and a clear statement of what it does and doesn't show.
