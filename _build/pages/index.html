Research

  • Sep – Oct 2026
    Detection Is Not Containment · first author, Apart sprint report, plus a solo follow-upI asked whether a monitor's alarm can come early enough to act on. In a small test environment it arrived just in time, and acting on it stopped the harmful action. When I scaled the setup up, the monitor mostly flagged problems too late. A follow-up showed that a simple change to the monitor's prompt helped, and that two of my own predictions were wrong.
  • Oct 2026
    Collusion among LLM agents · two team sprint reportsCan AI agents tell when a partner has been secretly replaced? In the study I led, a monitor quietly took over one colluding agent's seat. The collusion stopped, and the other agent never noticed. On a separate team project, I ran the probing and steering experiments on a pricing model.
  • May – Oct 2026
    LLM-generated world models · PRISM fellowship, five-author paperCan language models write world models that planning systems can use? Almost all the generated programs ran, but most got the underlying structure wrong. I built the evaluation pipeline and the test suite. Our paper was accepted as a poster at a NeurIPS 2026 workshop.

    Hyperspectral BiorNet · first authorMy first research paper, from before I moved into AI safety: a wavelet framework for hyperspectral unmixing, published at IEEE InGARSS 2025.
