Gagana M D · AI safety researcher · Bengaluru, India

I study how to monitor AI agents when we can't see everything they do.

Hi, I'm Gagana. I study whether the evidence we collect about AI agents is good enough to act on: whether a monitor notices a problem early enough to stop it, what agents' behaviour does and doesn't tell us about each other, and whether environments written by language models encode the situation we asked for.

Lately I've been thinking about how to test these ideas beyond one environment or one small model, which is where most of my results still come from. I'm currently working on behavioural evaluation and post-AGI governance through the Sentient Futures Project Incubator.

I'm looking for a funded research fellowship, a research engineering or research assistant role, or a PhD position in empirical AI safety.
